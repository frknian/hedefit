How it works

Get started in three steps.

  1. 1

    Start

    Start as a guest without signing up, or create an account. Answer seven short questions: your goal, days per week, time available, where you train and with which equipment.

  2. 2

    Choose your goal

    Lose weight, build muscle or maintain your shape. Hedefit calculates your weekly pace, finish date, and daily calorie and macro targets from your profile.

  3. 3

    Your program is ready

    Your personal program, animated exercises and Fit Coach are ready. Start a workout, add a meal, track your progress.

AI and data

AI helps, data does the math.

AI takes on the tiring jobs: reading a plate from a photo, reviewing your week, answering your questions, recognizing a machine at the gym. But critical numbers are not left to it: exercises in your program come from a verified atlas, meal calories from a database, and your goals from scientific formulas.

01

Type or photograph your meal

Type “2 slices of bread, omelette”: portions and calories are calculated from a Turkish-cuisine database. Or photograph your plate: AI recognizes the foods and estimates the amount. Either way you can choose grams or pieces and edit before saving.

02

Builds your program

“Create with Hedefit AI” builds a personal program from your goal, level, equipment and profile. It picks exercises from a verified atlas of 600+ moves, and AI explains the reasoning in a personal note. Even if you only have dumbbells or only a resistance band at home, you get a full-body program; it refreshes weekly or monthly while your main lifts stay fixed for your progress. Quick workouts, ready templates and your own programs are there too.

03

Fit Coach knows you

Questions like “What's my workout today?” or “What should I eat tonight?” are answered from your own workout, nutrition and progress records, not generic advice. It remembers important details; you can see and delete its memory.

04

Reviews your week

The weekly review summarizes what went well, what to watch and suggestions for next week, always with a safety note.

05

Recognizes equipment from a photo

Take a photo of a machine at the gym; Hedefit identifies the equipment and helps you find suitable exercises for your program.

06

AI doesn't make up your goal

Your daily calorie and macro targets are calculated from your profile with formulas, and activity calories with MET values. AI works only where an estimate is needed (portions from photos, comments, suggestions), so your targets stay consistent and realistic.

01

Workout

Today's workout waits on the home screen. Every exercise comes with an animated preview; during the workout you mark your sets with one tap or log them in detail.

  • Quick mode: tap sets and the rest timer starts on its own; the card turns green when complete
  • Detailed mode: exercise animation, weight and reps, effort (RPE), set type (warm-up, super, drop…) and stopwatch
  • An atlas of 600+ exercises with muscle and equipment filters
  • Muscle atlas: see which area you trained and how much this week
  • Quick workouts, ready programs, your own program or an AI program
  • A full-body program even with only dumbbells or only a resistance band at home
  • A program that refreshes weekly or monthly: main lifts stay fixed, accessories rotate; you're notified when a new block starts

02

Nutrition

Calorie ring, protein–carb–fat bars and water tracking on one screen. Type your meal and its calories are calculated from a Turkish-cuisine database; or photograph your plate.

  • Smart meal add by text or photo
  • About 300 food and drink entries: by grams, pieces, spoons, bowls or slices
  • If we're unsure about a value (e.g. did you weigh the rice raw or cooked?), we tell you why before you save
  • Calorie adjustment for workout days and activity
  • Fiber and micronutrient tracking, weekly meal plan

    Privacy and trust

    Your data stays yours.

    Health data is sensitive. We built Hedefit accordingly.

    We don't sell it

    Your data is never sold. Your health and nutrition data is not shared for advertising.

    Deleting is one tap

    You can permanently delete your account and all data tied to it from the app. If you prefer, freeze it first or reset only your progress data.

    Location only on routes

    Your location is used only when you start a recording with Hedefit Routes, and only for the duration of the recording.

    Your photos aren't stored

    Meal and equipment photos are processed only for analysis; they are not stored on our servers.

    AI doesn't make up your goal

    Your calorie and macro targets are calculated from your profile with formulas. AI works where an estimate is needed, and your data is not used to train models.

    The social side is yours

    Friends see only your XP and activity summary; your meals and measurements stay hidden. You can hide yourself from username search if you like.

    FAQ

    Is Hedefit free?

    Yes, you can start for free. Workout, nutrition and progress tracking are free. You get 3 Fit Coach questions a day as a guest and 5 on a free account; XP you earn from quests increases this. Plus and Premium plans offer higher limits, all exercises and an ad-free experience; see details on the plans page. Prices will be published on the store pages.

    Where is my data and who can see it?

    Data tied to your account (workouts, meals, routes, measurements) is stored in a secure cloud database and, through row-level access rules, is open only to your account. Your friends see only your XP and activity summary; your meal and measurement records stay hidden. For AI features (meal analysis from photos, Fit Coach, weekly review, equipment recognition), the information needed for a reply (the text you wrote or the photo you chose, plus a short summary of your profile, workouts and nutrition records) is sent to the AI provider. Meals you add by typing are mostly calculated from Hedefit's own food database; if an estimate is needed for a food not in the database, the text you typed may also go to the provider. Fit Coach may keep short notes in narrow categories such as your goal, preferences and constraints in its “memory”; these notes are deleted when you delete your account, and if you want them removed without deleting, you can write to the support address. Your username can be searched by other users who type at least 2 characters; you can turn this off with the “Appear in search” setting on the Friends screen.

    How do I delete my account and data?

    In the app, follow Profile and settings → Delete account permanently . Deletion is permanent. Before deleting you can freeze your account or reset only your progress data. For details, see the account deletion page .

    Can I try it without an account?

    Yes. You can start as a guest and save your account whenever you like so your progress isn't lost.

    Does Hedefit give medical advice?

    No. Hedefit is a fitness and habit-tracking app; it does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health condition, consult your doctor before starting a program.

    Will it work on a smartwatch?

    Hedefit's own watch app is in development: adding water, workout sets, the rest timer and cardio recording will be controllable from your wrist on Wear OS and Apple Watch. In addition, on Android your watch's health data can be transferred to the phone app via Health Connect (Samsung, Garmin, Xiaomi, Amazfit, Pixel Watch / Fitbit).

    Does it work on tablets and foldable phones?

    Yes. On tablets the workout screen opens in “stand mode”, split side by side in landscape and stacked in portrait. Foldable phones switch to the wide layout (side navigation bar) when unfolded; stand mode opens only on tablets.

    Which platforms will it be on?

    Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). The app offers a Turkish and English interface.
