    FAQ

    No matching question. You can write to us from the support page.

    Is Hedefit free?

    Yes, you can start for free. Workout, nutrition and progress tracking are free. You get 3 Fit Coach questions a day as a guest and 5 on a free account; XP you earn from quests increases this. Plus and Premium plans offer higher limits, all exercises and an ad-free experience; see details on the plans page. Prices will be published on the store pages.

    Where is my data and who can see it?

    Data tied to your account (workouts, meals, routes, measurements) is stored in a secure cloud database and, through row-level access rules, is open only to your account. Your friends see only your XP and activity summary; your meal and measurement records stay hidden. For AI features (meal analysis from photos, Fit Coach, weekly review, equipment recognition), the information needed for a reply (the text you wrote or the photo you chose, plus a short summary of your profile, workouts and nutrition records) is sent to the AI provider. Meals you add by typing are mostly calculated from Hedefit's own food database; if an estimate is needed for a food not in the database, the text you typed may also go to the provider. Fit Coach may keep short notes in narrow categories such as your goal, preferences and constraints in its “memory”; these notes are deleted when you delete your account, and if you want them removed without deleting, you can write to the support address. Your username can be searched by other users who type at least 2 characters; you can turn this off with the “Appear in search” setting on the Friends screen.

    How do I delete my account and data?

    In the app, follow Profile and settings → Delete account permanently . Deletion is permanent. Before deleting you can freeze your account or reset only your progress data. For details, see the account deletion page .

    Can I try it without an account?

    Yes. You can start as a guest and save your account whenever you like so your progress isn't lost.

    Does Hedefit give medical advice?

    No. Hedefit is a fitness and habit-tracking app; it does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health condition, consult your doctor before starting a program.

    Will it work on a smartwatch?

    Hedefit's own watch app is in development: adding water, workout sets, the rest timer and cardio recording will be controllable from your wrist on Wear OS and Apple Watch. In addition, on Android your watch's health data can be transferred to the phone app via Health Connect (Samsung, Garmin, Xiaomi, Amazfit, Pixel Watch / Fitbit).

    Does it work on tablets and foldable phones?

    Yes. On tablets the workout screen opens in “stand mode”, split side by side in landscape and stacked in portrait. Foldable phones switch to the wide layout (side navigation bar) when unfolded; stand mode opens only on tablets.

    Which platforms will it be on?

    Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). The app offers a Turkish and English interface.
