FAQ

What's the difference between Plus and Premium?

Premium offers higher daily allowances for Fit Coach (40 questions) and photo calorie scans (8), unlimited custom programs, regional programs and unlimited nutrition history. Plus comes with 20 questions, 3 photos, 5 programs and 90 days of history, enough for most users.

How do I pay and cancel?

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play or the App Store; to cancel, use the store's Subscriptions section. Deleting your account may not cancel the subscription automatically, so cancel in the store first.

When will prices be announced?

When Hedefit is published, prices will appear on the Google Play and App Store pages and will be updated on this page too.

What's in the free version?

On Guest and Free accounts, workout, nutrition and progress tracking, Fit Coach and photo calorie scans come with limited daily allowances. Ads are shown.
